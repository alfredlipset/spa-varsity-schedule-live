Section 3A Tournament

View Official Section 3A Bracket

Official MSHSL tournament bracket, matchups and results · Opens in a new tab.

Season final: SPA 1–2 Minnehaha Academy. SPA finished its season in the October 19 Section 3A semifinal at Lang Field. Final record: 13–5 · 45 goals for, 14 goals against.

Tuesday, October 13 · First round

High seed hosts. No. 10 Trinity School at No. 7 Math and Science Academy; No. 9 New Life Academy at No. 8 Nova Classical Academy. SPA does not play this round.

Thursday, October 15 · Quarterfinals

High seed hosts. Winner of 8/9 at No. 1 Holy Angels; No. 2 SPA defeated No. 10 Trinity School 7–0; No. 6 Minnehaha Academy at No. 3 St. Paul Humboldt; No. 5 St. Croix Lutheran at No. 4 Hiawatha Collegiate.

Monday, October 19 · Semifinals

Final: No. 2 SPA 1–2 No. 6 Minnehaha Academy. October 19 at Lang Field. Watch the final game.

Wednesday, October 21 · Final

5:30 PM at Saint Thomas Academy. SPA will not participate following its semifinal loss; Minnehaha Academy advances. See the official bracket for the championship matchup.
